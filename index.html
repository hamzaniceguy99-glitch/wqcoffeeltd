How we work

A Sussex company, straight about what's in the cup

WQ Coffee Ltd is a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 15635406, incorporated in April 2024, with its registered office at the Sussex Innovation Centre, Science Park Square, Brighton BN1 9SB.

We make no health or nutritional claims about anything we serve. Allergen information is available on request, and because drinks are prepared in an environment handling milk, soya, nuts and gluten, nothing can be promised free of traces — tell us about an allergy and we'll give you a straight answer about what we can do.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Where can we find you?

[YOUR USUAL PITCH OR LOCATION] — [YOUR TRADING HOURS]. Event dates vary, so check before travelling.

How much coffee do we need for an event?

Ask us — it depends on the numbers, the length and the time of day. We'd rather give you a realistic figure than oversell.

What do you need at a venue?

[POWER AND ACCESS REQUIREMENTS]. Tell us about access and parking early; it changes what's possible.

Do you do alternatives to dairy?

Yes, and we treat them properly rather than as an afterthought. Tell us at the point of ordering.

How do you handle allergies?

Ask before ordering and we'll tell you exactly what's in a drink. We're honest that a shared setup can't guarantee no traces.

How far do you travel?

Brighton and across Sussex, and further by arrangement. Send the venue and date and we'll say straight away.

Got an event or a regular slot?

Send the date, the numbers and the venue. A written quote, free, within a day.
